This registry prevents two phase agents from editing the same authority boundary at once. A phase may read earlier outputs, but it may modify only the ownership rows below unless the phase plan’s Validate Contract explicitly expands the boundary.

## Shared files and coordination

- packages/domain/src/schemas.ts, packages/domain/src/canonical-hash.ts, and the asset/merchant conversion helpers are owned by Phase 01; later schema additions require a plan-supplement and a domain-contract regression test.
- packages/pact-sdk/src/addresses.ts and packages/pact-sdk/src/abi.ts are generated by Phase 02 and extended by Phase 03 only through the export script.
- config/networks/advance-testnet.json is the canonical network-readiness input and is written only after Phase 01 preflight verifies concrete values. Phase 07 may create `config/deployments/*` artifacts, but must parity-check them against the Phase 01 config and may not promote a deployment manifest to source of truth.
- scripts/preflight-testnet.mjs is owned by Phase 01. Phase 07 may wrap it from `scripts/preflight-live-lane.mjs`, but may not modify or rename it.
- supabase/functions/_shared is owned by Phase 04; Phase 05 consumes its event/log helpers without changing authentication or payment authority.
- process/context and the phase reports are process-owned files; later phases may append routing entries only through their own plan-supplement and must not rewrite the Phase 01 router or historical reports in place.
- package.json root scripts are Phase 01 contracts. Later phases may add scripts only through a plan-supplement that records ownership and preserves existing command semantics.
- No phase owns docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-08-pact-mvp-aicd-design.md. Any design change requires a new design review, not a silent plan edit.

## Safe parallelism

The scaffold is authored sequentially so dependencies are explicit. During execution, research and innovation work may run in parallel only when their file reads do not overlap. Contract deployment, proof submission, provider calls, database migrations, and live UI flows remain gated joins.
